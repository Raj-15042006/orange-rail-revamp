IndRailInfo
IndRailInfo is a modern web application that provides detailed Indian Railways timetable information, enriched with historical context, special railway events, and operational insights such as train crosses and overtakes during a journey.
Built using TypeScript, Vite, and CSS, the app focuses on accuracy, performance, and realism, making it useful for railway enthusiasts, simulation users, researchers, and serious timetable followers.
About the Project
IndRailInfo goes beyond conventional timetable apps.
In addition to standard scheduling data, it models real-world railway operations of Indian Railways, including:
Train crossings at loop lines
Overtakes by faster services
Estimated interaction stations and timings
Historical and operational background of routes and trains
This makes IndRailInfo especially suitable for:
Railfans & historians
Railway simulation users (MSTS / Open Rails)
Operations-based timetable analysis
Long-distance journey realism studies
